# Privacy

- An upload is kept on our server for a few days (7 by default) and then deleted; nothing is kept after that.
- What goes to Meta's Model API, and when, is listed on the upload form before the player agrees; nothing goes without
  that agreement, and the player confirms they are 18 or older.
- The spoken lines of a lesson are made by a text-to-speech service only when the player agreed to it at upload; only
  the words of the lesson are sent.
- The recorded demonstrations are sent to the page only as encrypted cuts of the bars a lesson teaches.
