    • Only film playing that is yours, or a player's who gave you their OK to film and test it.
    • The video goes to the Museverse Teacher's server, which runs the teacher there. SAM 3.1, which finds the keyboard and your hands, runs on Meta's Model API: the frames it analyses (5 a second, without the sound) go to Meta for that, under Meta's Model API terms, which are for people 18 or older.SAM 3.1, which finds the keyboard and your hands, runs on Replicate: the frames it analyses (5 a second, without the sound) go there for that.SAM 3.1, which finds the keyboard and your hands, runs on the server itself: no frame is sent anywhere. The notes are heard on the server; the sound is not sent anywhere.
    • The video and its lesson are kept on the server for a few days, then deleted.
    • Muse writes the words: Muse Wonder judges what to teach first and Muse Spark words it, both on Meta's Model API, from the teacher's measurements of your take (never the video or its sound). If the server cannot reach them, the teacher's fixed lines word the lesson and nothing goes to a language model.

                            What we built during the hackathon · How it works · Notices

                            How the teacher works

                            What is measured. For a demo, the Museverse Quest app recorded both hands (26 points each, up to 90 times a second) and every note straight from the piano. For your own video, the notes and their timing come from its sound, or from your piano's MIDI when you add it, and SAM 3.1 finds the keyboard and your hands in the frames. Which finger played, finger curvature and tension are not judged.

                            The voice is your browser's. Meta has no text-to-speech yet, so your browser reads the teacher's lines aloud. The wording and the answers come from Meta's Muse, our own Muse Glimmer server, or fixed lines, depending on this server and the take; each says which.

                            Privacy notice

                            Privacy notice for the Museverse Teacher beta

                            This notice covers the Museverse Teacher beta on this page. Museverse runs it.

                            What is collected

                            • The video you upload, with its sound, and its file name; the score file, if you add one; and the MIDI your piano recorded while you filmed, if you add it. That MIDI file stays on this server: its notes become your lesson's notes when it can be lined up with the video.
                            • What the analysis makes from it: frames from the video (5 a second), the notes heard, where the keyboard and your hands are, and the teacher's remarks.
                            • The questions you type or speak to the teacher. Your browser sends each one with the conversation so far; this server answers it and does not keep it, or the recording of a spoken one.
                            • That you ticked the agreement before uploading and, when it is asked, that you are 18 or older. Before your first spoken question, that you are 18 or older and agreed that your voice goes to Meta. The server also logs requests and their times.
                            • An access token or a judge code, if this server asks for one, is kept in your browser only, and so is the key to each take you upload with a judge code.
                            • The list of your takes on the home page (each one's link, the video's file name, its piece, the day, the part analysed and what was found) is kept in your browser only: nothing about it is sent to this server. Remove a take from it, or clear it, there; forgetting the access token and judge code on this device clears it too.
                            • When you film part of a take again, which take it films again, so the two can be compared. The comparison holds your first take's numbers, and it opens for whoever can open the re-take: the access token, the judge code both were uploaded with, or the re-take's key in this browser.

                            Where it is processed

                            On this server. Your browser reads the teacher's lines aloud with its own voice. If the mic uses your browser's own speech recognition, your browser may send your voice to its maker.

                            The demos

                            The demos are takes recorded in the Museverse Quest app. Nothing made from them, including what you ask about them, is sent to Meta's hosted Muse unless Meta has agreed to it in writing; this server's settings are being checked.

                            The sample lessons

                            The sample lessons are phone videos our team filmed, shown with each player's agreement. A question you type about one goes to a hosted AI model only when that sample's player agreed to it, you tick that you are 18 or older, and this server uses one; the line under the question box says who answers. A sample's video uploaded again is still its player's take: their agreement holds for it, whatever boxes are ticked, and its frames stay on this server. This server does not keep your questions; it counts them for a few minutes, by your address, to limit how many one visitor can ask.

                            How long it is kept

                            Your upload, its score and its MIDI files, and everything made from them are deleted from this server a limited time after you upload it, and so is the record of your agreement to spoken questions. Until then, anyone with the result's link and this server's access token can open it, and so can anyone with the link and the judge code it was uploaded with, if it was.

                            Age

                            When this server uses Meta's Model API, you must be 18 or older to upload, and to ask the teacher out loud through Meta's speech-to-text.

                            Deletion and questions

                            To have a take deleted sooner, or with any question about your data, email hello@museverse.xyz with the job number shown on your result.
